Going Bananas...for Murder!

By

Author Unknown

INT. APARTMENT LIVING ROOM - DAY

FADE IN

(Enter Belle Weather carrying a banana and Doug Chambers carrying nothing)

BELLE

(To the banana) This is really going to screw up my marching band practice if you don't quit!

DOUG

(Following her) It's a banana, Belle, what harm could it do?

BELLE

It's been staring at me all day. I'm trying to do my trombone steps, but it keeps looking at me, mocking me. Waiting for me to slip up. And really, just staring!

DOUG

How could a banana stare at you?

BELLE

Like this! (points banana at Doug)

DOUG

(A beat) Is that it?

BELLE

Aren't you scared?

DOUG

Of that banana staring at me?

BELLE

Well, it's not going to listen to you to death, Doug.

DOUG

If there's anyone's eyes we should be scared of, it's a potato's, ha! There's an Eldritch fright right there!

BELLE

This is not a joking matter, Doug!

DOUG

That's not even where a banana's eyes would go.

BELLE

Hum, I'm just covering its real eyes to protect you.

DOUG

Then move your hand.

BELLE

I can't do it.

DOUG

Why not?

BELLE

I don't want that evil on you, too.

DOUG

This conversation is losing its appeal.

BELLE

Alright, fine. Do you really want me to move my hand so you can see the horror?

DOUG

Try it and see what happens.

BELLE

Oh no, I just realized, if I move my hand, I'll drop it, and then it will be really mad, and then we'll all be doomed!

DOUG

Belle?

BELLE

Yeah, Doug?

DOUG

That's what your other hand is for.

BELLE

Huh?

DOUG

Put your other hand on the other end first. Then open your first hand.

BELLE

Oh, right, right!

(Belle hesitates)

DOUG

What's the matter?

BELLE

I'm hesitating!

DOUG

What are ya, yellow?

BELLE

Don't joke about yellow! Ugh, fine!

(Belle places her other hand, then opens the first one, but still slightly covers this part of the banana so it is unseen by the audience.)

DOUG

Oh my God! Close your hands! Close them!

(Belle closes them quickly)

BELLE

See what I mean?

DOUG

What the Fresno was that?

BELLE

I told you!

DOUG

Are you sure you didn't just put googly eyes on it?

BELLE

What?

DOUG

Or maybe even just marker them on?

BELLE

Do you want to take a closer look to see if these look markered to you!? (holds banana menacingly closer to DOUG'S face)

DOUG

Okay! Okay! Look, we've got to get to the stem of the problem. And I think there's only one person who can solve this.

BELLE

Who?

DOUG

The resident chef of this apartment.

BELLE

Yes, of course! The one who fixes all our meals, because we can't!

DOUG

And the only other person who lives here!

BELLE

The one!

DOUG

The only!

BELLE &AMP; DOUG

(together) Joseph Grossman!

(enter JOSEPH GROSSMAN)

JOSEPH

You rang?

BELLE

What? (looks around, then at the banana) Does this look like a phone to you!? (shakes banana)

JOSEPH

Ooh. Banana? I love banana! (starts walking towards the banana)

BELLE

Stop where you are!

JOSEPH

Woah, why the hostility, Belle?

DOUG

We got a bunch of problems on our hands!

BELLE

But mainly this one!

(She opens her first hand slightly again)

JOSEPH

(looks closely) Um, what's the problem?

BELLE

Oops, wrong one. (opens the correct hand this time)

JOSEPH

Oh my God, that banana is nuts! It just stares into your soul. Like those eyes are preparing to plant the very seeds of evil, itself!

BELLE

I know right? It's been tallying my every move! Trying to catch me trippin', or worse!

JOSEPH

Where did it come from?

BELLE

I was just cleaning my pajamas when I happened on two bananas in the kitchen. I thought why not and took one with me. But for some reason it bruised his ego, or something.

JOSEPH

Oh. Oh no.

DOUG

Joseph? Are you turning green?

JOSEPH

If there's only one rule I know about evil bananas, is that you should never split them! At least not without the assistance of strawberries and pineapples. A second banana is loose in this house, even more evil than the first, and he is hungry for revenge!

BELLE

Oh no no no!

DOUG

To paraphrase a famous song, this situation is bananas!

BELLE

Can't I just throw it back into the kitchen? I'm getting more and more disturbed about holding it!

JOSEPH

No! It's eyes are only growing more powerful, the longer you are covering them!

BELLE

Well, I'm fresh out of ideas, then.

DOUG

Wait! I know how we can cover its eyes forever!

BELLE

How?

DOUG

I have a roll of duct tape. Just wrap it and stick it on the wall!

BELLE

That's a million dollar idea, Doug!

JOSEPH

Wait! What about the second banana?

BELLE

Doug! Remember what you were saying about the evil potato with even more eyes?

DOUG

Yes! Well no, but I like where you're going with this.

BELLE

Get that potato and turn it on the other banana.

JOSEPH

I love it when a plantain comes together!

DOUG

But wait again! I only have regular potatoes. I don't know how to make them evil.

BELLE

I believe in you. I believe in all of us. C'mon let's get them!

(They all EXIT)

BELLE, DOUG, &AMP; JOSEPH

(together) Aaaaah! Third banana!

(SFX of triple banana peel slipping)

FADE OUT
